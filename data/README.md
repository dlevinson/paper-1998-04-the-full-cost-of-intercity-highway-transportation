# Data Boundary

This folder contains the paper-specific highway Full Cost data/model materials for:

Levinson, David M., and David Gillen. (1998). "The Full Cost of Intercity Highway Transportation." Transportation Research Part D: Transport and Environment 3(4):207-223. https://doi.org/10.1016/S1361-9209(97)00037-0

The retained data are aggregate/model/statistical materials from the Full Cost Project C4 Highway work and the HIGHWAYS archive. They include legacy workbooks, statistical inputs/outputs, and modernized XLSX/CSV sidecars where conversion was successful.

Repeated Full Cost report and social-cost model files are not stored here. They are referenced through `_shared_sources/full-cost-intercity-transportation-1996/` and listed in `../data/DEDUP_POINTER_MANIFEST.csv`.
